2.0 Pill Tab and Status Mark. Every `TabsList` variant now draws the Figma Pill Tab: 28 tall, a 6px corner, the chosen tab on the neutral 8% fill with body ink. The line indicator and the segmented track are gone; `variant="line"` still builds and draws the same pill. `StatusOrb` draws the Figma Status Mark: round marks in the shared status colours, a quarter arc turning over a faint track for working, a hollow ring for idle, and a disc with a check or a cross for done and failed.
