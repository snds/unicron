Unicron 2.0 foundations, from the published Figma library (2026-11-24): a theme is the accent only (10 slots); shared neutrals in four surface layers with alpha lines, hover and selection; status, count, severity, label and chart roles; the radius scale (4, 6, 8, 12, 16, round); app-sized type in one sans with seven new roles; popover and dialog shadows and the frost blur. The legacy ramps are gone. See docs/10-migrating-to-2.0.md.
