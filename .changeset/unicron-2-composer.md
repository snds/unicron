`Composer` takes the 2.0 library's Composer (Figma 32:91), the floating raised bar. It has 16px corners and a 1px line/field edge, and is 88 tall at rest. The field sits on top in the body role. Under it is a tools row: `attach` and `leadingAction` first, then Send at the end, now a 32px circle on the accent fill with an up arrow. The bar is padded 12 at the top and right, 10 at the bottom, and 16 on the left. The one-line size keeps a 24px field.
