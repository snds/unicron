New `TitleRow`, from the 2.0 library (Figma Title Row). The 48px desktop title row every app shares: room for the Mac window buttons, the sidebar toggle, the breadcrumb (the parent in secondary ink as a link back, a chevron, the current page), an optional status, then the actions and the panel toggle (⌘., with the needs-you dot). `kind="settings"` draws the Settings location label, "Settings | Page", with a 1 × 16 rule and a plain parent.
